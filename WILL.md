# Maintainer's Will for maintainers-will

> **Note:** This is a v0.1 prototype (generated by Claude) and is **not yet in
> effect** — the successor below is a placeholder and the handover only runs as
> a dry run. It exists to demonstrate the format.

This tool has a bus factor too. If I (`@loopius`) go silent, this file says
what should happen to `maintainers-will` and who should take over. It is a
public promise, not a legal will — and it is here so the project practices what
it preaches (design principle: *the tool itself has a WILL.md*).

**What matters to me for this project**

- The license stays MIT.
- No hosting, no account, no telemetry — everything lives in the repo.
- Rights are never granted without an explicit human approval.

**For my successors**

`backup-maintainer` above is a placeholder. Before this file means anything,
replace it with people who already contribute here and who have confirmed their
nomination with `/accept-nomination` in the Will issue. Don't nominate someone
who isn't already involved — that is exactly the attack this project is built to
prevent.

**How this works**

A weekly GitHub Action measures whether I am still active. If I am not, it moves
through public stages — reminder at 90 days, warning at 180, handover after a
30-day grace — each with a visible deadline in the Will issue. A single sign of
life from me resets everything. In v0.1 the handover is a dry run: it records
the state and grants nothing.
